# Code of conduct

[Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

We welcome contributors from all backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep discussions respectful, technically grounded and reproducible.

Harassment, intimidation, threats, discriminatory behavior, unwanted disclosure of private information and personal attacks are unacceptable. Constructive criticism of engineering choices is welcome.

Report concerns privately to project maintainers; do not post victims' private information or secrets in public issues. Maintainers may hide abusive content, close discussions or restrict access, with proportionate action.
